# The link in your bio

`your-address/bio` is a small, phone-first page made for the one link a social profile allows. It is generated from what you have already entered, so there is no second list of links to maintain.

It shows:

- your portrait, name and role;
- the first paragraph of your About story, if there is one;
- buttons for your main portfolio and your CV;
- up to three **featured** projects that are published;
- a contact button;
- your visible social profiles.

Change which projects are featured on the **Selected work** screen. Change your portrait, name, role, About story or social links in their usual screens, and `/bio` follows.

The page uses the same theme and colour tokens as the rest of your site. Nothing on it is a separate design to keep in step.

Paste `your-address/bio` into the website field of your social profile. The main portfolio remains at `/`.
